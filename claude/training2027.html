Training Overview

Commute framing is gone — the structure that shaped every weekday in the last two drafts. In its place: Hills R Us anchors Wednesday the way WW LDT anchors Saturday.

What Hills R Us Changes

  • What it isWestern Wheelers' Wednesday club ride. Meet 8:45, roll 9:00 — DE group departs first, C+ follows. Route rotates Hwy 9, Page Mill, Old La Honda, Kings Mountain, West Alpine, Tunitas Creek, announced via the WW rides list the Monday prior. Foul weather in the hills keeps the ride in the valley — it self-adjusts for conditions.
  • Bigger than the placeholder it replacesThe old "2–2.5 hr Wednesday ride" was sized for a commute-constrained day. HRU's actual route menu runs closer to 3+ hours with real climbing — durations and TSS targets below are adjusted up accordingly, especially in Base.
  • No dedicated test daysFormal 20-min all-out tests are gone this cycle — limited value in 2026 (the test result routinely got overridden by context anyway) and tied to an injury. Old La Honda's regular appearances in the rotation still give an honest read on fitness whenever you hit it hard within the group, with zero added risk since the ride was happening regardless.
  • One ride, many rolesSame route pool, different intent by phase: capped effort in Foundation, controlled Z2 early Base, Sweet Spot/threshold intervals on its climbs through Build, and in Peak it doubles directly as the Alps Day Simulation — multiple climbs back to back is exactly what a tour day asks for.

Planning Assumptions — Correct These If Wrong

  • No commuteEvery weekday is fully flexible now. Monday/Tuesday/Thursday are standalone rides, not fragmented commute legs, which if anything makes structured intervals easier to execute properly (full warmup/cooldown, chosen terrain, no traffic-light interruptions).
  • Training startAssumed Sat Jan 2, 2027 — first Saturday of the year, standing in for the actual LDT season opener.
  • Fly-out dateThursday, not Saturday. Boen's school ends Wed Jun 2 — nearest Thursday is Jun 3, a 1-day buffer. Jun 10 is the fallback if too tight.
  • Where the weeks wentRound 1 (11→15 weeks) extended Base and Build. Round 2 (15→22 weeks) added Foundation. This round (weekday restructure) doesn't change week count — same 22 weeks, same dates, just no commute language and HRU as the Wednesday anchor.
  • No dedicated FTP testsDropped this round. 2026's tests had limited standalone value — the Apr 2 result got overridden by context within the same paragraph it was recorded in, and the real signal all cycle came from regular interval efforts, not the dedicated test days. One of those tests is also tied to an injury. Working FTP holds at 199W, nudged only if HRU/LDT data clearly says it's off — no maximal-effort day required to find that out.

Strength Training

Five phases, unchanged by the weekday restructure. No commute meant strength timing was already flexible; nothing here needed adjusting.

Phase 0: Anatomical Adaptation (Jan 2 – Feb 19)

  • Sets × Reps: 2–3×12–15 @ 50–60% 1RM or bodyweight
  • Focus: Movement quality, joint/tendon prep, injury-proofing
  • Rest: 60–75 sec
  • Both sessions: Bodyweight/light squats, glute bridges, planks, band walks, light step-ups, hip 90-90 and ankle/thoracic mobility.

Phase 1: Hypertrophy (Feb 20 – Apr 2)

  • Sets × Reps: 4×8–10 @ 70–75% 1RM
  • Rest: 90–120 sec
  • Mon (Sess A): Back Squat, RDL, Step-Up, Hip Thrust, Plank
  • Thu (Sess B): Single-Leg Press, Nordic Curl, Lateral Band Walk, Pull-Ups, Dead Bug

Phase 2: Power (Apr 3 – May 7)

  • Sets × Reps: 3×5–6 @ 80–85% 1RM, explosive intent
  • Rest: 2–3 min
  • Mon (Sess A): Back Squat (explosive), Box Jump 3×5, RDL, Hip Thrust, Pallof Press
  • Thu (Sess B): Single-Leg Box Step-Down, Nordic Curl, Jump Squat 3×5, Row, Copenhagen Plank

Phase 3: Maintenance (May 8 – May 21)

  • Sets × Reps: 2×12 @ 60–65% 1RM
  • Rest: 60–90 sec
  • Both sessions: Squat, RDL, Step-Up, Single-Leg Press, Core.

Phase 4: Activation (May 22 – Jun 3)

  • No loaded lifting in the final 13 days.
  • Bodyweight only: Air squats 2×20, Glute bridges 2×20, Single-leg balance, Hip 90-90 stretching
  • Duration: 15–20 min max.
